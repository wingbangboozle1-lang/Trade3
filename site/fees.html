Fees decide whether a strategy is real. These are the numbers the paper trader charges and the numbers the scoring model uses, transcribed from Kalshi's official fee schedule and cross-checked against the published table.

The formula, verbatim

Taker: fees = roundup(M x 0.07  x C x P x (1-P))
Maker: fees = roundup(M x 0.0175 x C x P x (1-P))

P = price per contract in dollars (0.50 = 50 cents)
C = number of contracts
M = per-contract multiplier (taker default 1, maker default 0)
round up = fee + position cost rounded to a centicent ($0.0001)

Source: K6 · rounding detail: K7

There is no settlement fee and no membership fee. A resting order is not charged until it trades, and cancelling a resting order is free. K6

Worked example

Buy 100 YES at $0.45 as a taker. Taker fee = 0.07 × 100 × 0.45 × 0.55 = $1.7325, charged at centicent granularity. The published table prints $1.74 because it rounds to whole cents. If the same order rested and filled as a maker at the general 0× multiplier, the maker fee would be $0.00.

This asymmetry is the entire basis of the maker strategy in the dashboard, and it is why the taker strategy is included as a control: it shows the cost of impatience.
