Where these numbers come from

  • Categories, tags and settlement sources: GET /series and GET /series/{ticker} (docs).
  • Prices, bid/ask, volume, open interest: GET /markets (docs).
  • Settled results (yes/no): the result field on settled markets (settlement docs).
  • “$ traded” is contracts × last price — a disclosed approximation, because Kalshi publishes contract counts rather than dollar volume (F4).
