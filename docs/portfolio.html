Simulated portfolio & strategy lab

Everything here is simulated. Trades are priced from live Kalshi demo quotes, never sent to the exchange, and never marked as realised until the exchange publishes a result. Real demo execution needs your own demo API key — the signing client is in the repo (kalshi_client.py) and is documented on the Method page.

Fill model: buy at the quoted ask, mark at the last trade price, settle at $1.00 (yes) or $0.00 (no). No fees, no slippage beyond the quoted book — stated plainly so results are reproducible.

Reading the results honestly

  • One snapshot is not a backtest. This page replays strategies against the current market set. The Python backtest (run_sim.py) samples repeatedly over time; use it for anything you intend to act on.
  • Demo prices are synthetic. Kalshi states demo market behaviour may not reflect real markets, so demo P&L is a plumbing test, not an edge measurement.
  • Unrealised P&L is not profit. It is a mark against the last trade price and will move.
  • Strategy rules are public and fixed (shown above), so you can judge them rather than trust them.
