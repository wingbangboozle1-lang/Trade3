Gas Prices

Retail gasoline price thresholds. Kalshi series KXAAAGASM resolves against AAA national average regular gasoline prices.

How markets reach this page

Routed by official metadata only:

  • category = Economics — too broad to use alone, since it also covers CPI, GDP and eggs.
  • tags contains Oil & Gas or Oil and energy — this is what identifies gas.
  • product_metadata.scope = Gasoline.

There is no Gas category on Kalshi. GET /series?category=Gas returns {"series":null}, so a category filter would show nothing.

What to expect in the demo.

Gas markets are monthly threshold ladders. In the demo, most strikes are quoted at $0.00–$0.03 with empty order books, so a $0.10 swing is rare until real trading activity exists.

Why a swing alert may not fire here

The alert rule is absolute: a move of $0.10 or more, either direction. If a market sits at $0.00 with an empty book, its price cannot move, so no alert is possible. That is a property of the demo environment, which Kalshi warns is not representative of real markets. The dashboard shows its data mode at all times so an empty feed is never mistaken for a working live feed.
