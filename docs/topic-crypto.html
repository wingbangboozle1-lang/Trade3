Bitcoin and altcoin price thresholds. Crypto markets settle on CF Benchmarks real-time indices, not on exchange screenshots.

How markets reach this page

Routed by official category Crypto, or by tags such as Crypto, Bitcoin or Hourly.

What to expect in the demo.

These are hourly threshold ladders with dozens of strikes. At capture time most strikes were quoted at $0.00 with empty books. Kalshi explicitly warns that the price used to settle is the CF Benchmarks average of the final 60 seconds — a Google or Coinbase quote is not the settlement value.

Why a swing alert may not fire here

The alert rule is absolute: a move of $0.10 or more, either direction. If a market sits at $0.00 with an empty book, its price cannot move, so no alert is possible. That is a property of the demo environment, which Kalshi warns is not representative of real markets. The dashboard shows its data mode at all times so an empty feed is never mistaken for a working live feed.
