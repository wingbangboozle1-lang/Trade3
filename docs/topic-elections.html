Elections

Elections, primaries, governorships and international races. Elections is a first-class Kalshi category.

How markets reach this page

Routed by official category Elections, or by tags such as Governor, US Elections, Primaries or International Elections.

Note that many election markets carry long lists of trading prohibitions — for example holders of federal or statewide office, paid campaign staff, and anyone working in vote tallying cannot trade them. Those prohibitions are contractual, not advisory.

What to expect in the demo.

Several Elections series had no open markets at capture time (for example GOVPARTYVA, the Virginia Governor series). Open election markets were found under the NATO Secretary General event instead.

Why a swing alert may not fire here

The alert rule is absolute: a move of $0.10 or more, either direction. If a market sits at $0.00 with an empty book, its price cannot move, so no alert is possible. That is a property of the demo environment, which Kalshi warns is not representative of real markets. The dashboard shows its data mode at all times so an empty feed is never mistaken for a working live feed.
