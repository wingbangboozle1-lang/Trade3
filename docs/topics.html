Topics

You asked for five topics only: gas prices, elections, culture, crypto and golf. Keeping them separate is easy; defining them honestly is not, because Kalshi's category vocabulary does not contain “gas” or “golf” at all. Each page below states exactly which official fields route a market into that topic.

The routing problem, stated plainly. GET /series?category=Gas and ?category=Golf both return {"series":null}. Gas markets actually sit in category Economics with the tag Oil & Gas and a product scope of Gasoline. Golf markets sit in category Sports with the tag Golf. Anything that filters on a “Gas” or “Golf” category shows an empty page. Verify it yourself: ?category=Gas · ?category=Golf.

The five topics

Routing rules, in priority order

  1. Official product scope or tag — Gasoline scope, or a Golf / Oil & Gas tag. These decide gas and golf, because their Kalshi categories are far too broad.
  2. Official series category — Crypto, Elections and Culture map one-to-one to a topic.
  3. Other official tags — for example a Governor tag under an Elections category.
  4. Whole-word keyword fallback on the market title, used only when official metadata is missing. Whole-word matching exists because naive substring matching produced real false positives: “Best Picture” contains “art”, and “participation” contains “par”.
  5. Series ticker prefix — for example KXPGATOUR… or KXAAAGASM….

If nothing matches, the market is dropped from the dashboard. Nothing is ever forced into a topic bucket, and every routed market records which rule matched so you can audit it.
