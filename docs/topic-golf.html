Golf

PGA Tour tournament winners, round leaders and cuts. Kalshi files golf under Sports, distinguished only by its tag.

How markets reach this page

Routed by the official tag Golf. The category is Sports, which also covers NFL, NBA, MLB and NCAA games, so the category alone cannot identify golf.

There is no Golf category: GET /series?category=Golf returns {"series":null}.

What to expect in the demo.

Golf markets publish different resolution rules by market type. For tournament winner markets, a golfer who withdraws or does not tee off resolves to NO; finishing-position, make-the-cut and head-to-head markets resolve to fair market value instead.

Why a swing alert may not fire here

The alert rule is absolute: a move of $0.10 or more, either direction. If a market sits at $0.00 with an empty book, its price cannot move, so no alert is possible. That is a property of the demo environment, which Kalshi warns is not representative of real markets. The dashboard shows its data mode at all times so an empty feed is never mistaken for a working live feed.
