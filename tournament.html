Strategy tournament

no relay

One swing, four strategies. Every time the collector detects a move of ≥ $0.10 in either direction, each strategy is offered the same swing at the same quote, and each decides for itself whether to trade and which side to take. Results stay open until Kalshi publishes result: yes|no.

Buying YES costs the YES ask; buying NO costs 1 − best YES bid, because “a bid for yes at price X is equivalent to an ask for no at (1 − X)” (Get Market Orderbook). A winning contract pays $1.00 (Market Settlement). No fees are charged, because the demo environment's fee behaviour cannot be verified here without placing real demo orders — so treat every figure as gross, and see Flagged for that open item.

How to read this

  • Momentum follows the swing; mean reversion fades it. They are near-exact opposites, so they are a control on each other: when one makes money on the same events, the other loses it.
  • Threshold break only trades when the swing crosses $0.25, $0.50 or $0.75 — a deliberately selective rule, so expect far fewer trades.
  • Fade extreme only trades when a swing lands above $0.85 or below $0.15, testing the “overreaction at the edges” idea.
  • ¢ / contract is the average settled P&L per contract, which lets strategies that traded different sizes be compared honestly.
  • Nothing here is advice, and nothing is filled in. An empty cell means the exchange has not settled anything yet.
