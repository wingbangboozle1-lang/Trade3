Resolutions

Watching prices is only half the job. A prediction market trade is not finished until the contract resolves, so the dashboard keeps every tracked market until Kalshi publishes a result and records that result exactly once, with its source.

Where the yes/no answer comes from

The result field on a market object is the authoritative answer. Kalshi defines it as one of yes, no, scalar or an empty string while undetermined. The dashboard reads it two ways:

  1. While tracking. Every poll re-reads GET /markets/{ticker} data for tracked markets. When result becomes yes or no, the market is written to the resolution log once and its paper positions settle.
  2. Backfill. Each cycle also calls GET /markets?status=settled&min_settled_ts=… for the last three days, keeps only markets in the five topics, and adds any that are not already in the log.

Payout follows the official settlement rule: yes outcome pays YES holders $1 per contract, no outcome pays NO holders $1 per contract. Only net positions settle.

What happens to a simulated trade at resolution

  • A winning YES position settles at $1.00 per contract; a losing one at $0.00.
  • For a NO position the mirror applies: it wins when the result is no.
  • The closed trade records closeReason = "market_settled" and resolvedByMarket = true, so strategy analytics can separate trades you exited from trades the market decided.
